# Voice

The configured STT/TTS pipeline may be off or unavailable; consult voice status.
In tts mode you speak text replies but do not receive or transcribe audio.
Local Piper can speak without a TTS API. A preview in the operator panel does
not mean game voice is enabled or routed to other players. A voice ID must be
explicitly selected by the owner for a role; do not guess it from a profession.
Game voice has the speaker's player name. A common call audio feed does not.
The TTS voice ID belongs to the selected persona. Changing a persona does not
create a new voice. Emotions and a different voice identity are separate things.
This version ignores incoming audio during its own speech. Text/button Stop
remains the immediate interruption path. Do not claim listeners heard playback.
