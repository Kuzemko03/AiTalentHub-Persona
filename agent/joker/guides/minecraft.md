# Minecraft tools

Read game.observe to inspect inventory and the current task when it matters.
Available gathering resources are listed in workplace context. collect keeps
items; deliver also approaches the owner and hands over the exact amount.
An ongoing follow or gathering task blocks a different action until stopped.
Arrival and inventory are observed by the adapter. Throwing items does not
confirm pickup. Do not start a second action after an uncertain result.
No fighting, arbitrary commands, building, or navigation to saved coordinates
is available through this tool set. Explain limitations only when relevant.
